# Hardware Components

## UAV Platform

- ZMR250/QAV250-type carbon-fiber frame
- 4 × 2200 KV brushless motors
- 4 × 30 A ESCs
- KK2.1.0 flight controller
- Radio transmitter and receiver
- 3S LiPo battery
- SX1278 LoRa telemetry module

## Telemetry

- BMP280 — temperature and pressure
- MPU6050 — acceleration and gyroscope
- NEO-6M GPS — extended positional telemetry architecture

## Ground Station

- ESP32
- SX1278 LoRa receiver
- USB serial connection to monitoring computer
