# UAV Power System

Recommended starting battery:

- Type: LiPo
- Configuration: 3S
- Nominal voltage: 11.1 V
- Fully charged: 12.6 V
- Capacity: 1500 mAh
- Discharge rating: 40C
- Connector: XT60

The expected 4–6 minute flight time is an estimate dependent on final aircraft weight, propeller selection, battery condition, and flying style.

Use appropriate LiPo charging, storage, and flight safety procedures.
