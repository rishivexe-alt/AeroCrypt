## Reported Field Test

A separate reported approximately 500 m line-of-sight test recorded about 93% packet delivery, approximately -60 dBm RSSI, and approximately 5 dB SNR under the tested configuration. These figures correspond to a specific tested condition and should not be generalized to all environments.
